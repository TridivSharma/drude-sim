# drude-sim
drude-sim is an attempt to demonstrate how DC circuits actually work.

The mechanism behind how circuits work is often not taught well (or at all!), how is it that the current is roughly constant throughout the entire circuit? How is that the electric field guides the electrons along the wire no matter the shape? After all, the shape of the electric field should not depend on the shape of the wire so why is it that, in the high school experiment, they do not tell you to make sure your wire follows a particular contour?

I have not seen these questions being adressed, even in the free online courses provided by prestigious universities and that is a problem.
